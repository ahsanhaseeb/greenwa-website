The GreenWA way

Most paving doesn't fail at the top. It sinks, spreads and pools water because of what was *skipped* underneath. So we start where others stop: at the *ground.* Every layer is measured, compacted and checked before the next one goes down.

Base

Road base compacted in lifts, never just dumped and raked.

Fall

A built-in fall on every surface, so water runs away from the house.

Edges

Cuts and restrained edges, so pavers don't creep.

Questions

Asked on almost every site visit.

How long does a paving job take?

It depends on the size of the area, how much excavation is needed and the site access, so we don't promise a fixed finish date. We'll talk you through what to expect at the site visit.

Why does base preparation matter so much?

Perth sits on sand. Without properly compacted road base and a correct fall, pavers sink, spread and pond water within a couple of years. The layers you can't see decide how long the ones you can see last.

Do retaining walls need council approval?

Often, especially walls over 500 mm or close to a boundary. It depends on your local council, so we'll tell you what applies to your site and help with the paperwork.

When is the best time to lay turf in Perth?

Spring through early autumn, when the soil is warm and roots establish fast. Winter laying is possible for some varieties, but establishment is slower.

Can you combine services in one job?

Yes, and it usually saves time and cost. A common combo is a retaining wall to level the yard, then paving for the alfresco and turf for the rest. One crew, one schedule.

Which suburbs do you cover?

Perth metro only: from Yanchep in the north to Secret Harbour in the south, and out to the Hills.
